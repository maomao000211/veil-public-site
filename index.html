Frequently Asked Questions

Does Veil work with Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet?

Yes. Veil works alongside any screen sharing tool. Toggle the overlay before you share your screen. Note that Veil does not auto-detect when you start sharing—you enable it manually from the menu bar.

Is this the same Veil on the Mac App Store?

No. Veil for Mac (veilformac.com) is a separate product distributed as a signed, notarized DMG. The App Store app "Veil-Focus & Screen Privacy" is a different product by a different developer.

How do I activate my license?

After purchasing, open Veil from the menu bar, go to Settings, and enter your license key. The 3-day trial starts automatically when you first launch the app.

What macOS versions are supported?

Veil requires macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.

Is the DMG safe to install?

Yes. The DMG is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. macOS Gatekeeper will verify the signature when you open it.
